# Tino and Friends - Assets Folder

## Logo Setup

The Navbar is configured to display your logo image. To add the logo:

1. **Save your logo image** as `logo.png` in this folder (`frontend/public/assets/`)
   - The logo should be square or have equal width/height for best results
   - Recommended size: 200x200px or higher (it will be scaled to 45px in the navbar)
   - Format: PNG with transparent background works best

2. **The Navbar will automatically display:**
   - Logo image (45px height, circular with border-radius)
   - "Tino and Friends" text next to it
   - Warm, cozy styling matching your brand

3. **Refresh your browser** after adding the logo file
